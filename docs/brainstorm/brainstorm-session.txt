First, the basics

Escrow is a neutral middleman that holds money until a deal is done. Say you buy a used phone from a stranger online. You’re scared to pay first, and they’re scared to ship first. So you both give the money to a trusted holder. The seller ships, you confirm the phone is real, and the holder releases the money. If the seller never ships, you get a refund.

Upwork is a website where businesses hire freelancers. The client puts the payment in escrow before work starts, and the freelancer knows the money exists. When the work is approved, the money goes to the freelancer. Upwork keeps about 10% of every job for running the platform. That’s what I meant by a “small fee as revenue.” The platform doesn’t sell anything. It just takes a small cut of every job that passes through it.

On Monad, a smart contract plays the middleman, so no company has to hold the money.

Now, the bigger idea

You’re right that “escrow for AI agents” is something other teams will think of too. So here’s an umbrella that goes further.

Idea: a bank, credit score, and warranty for AI agents

Right now, if you hire an AI agent and it messes up, you lose your money and nobody is responsible. We fix that with three simple pieces:

Agent ID. Every agent gets an on-chain identity and a track record anyone can check (the track’s ERC-8004 theme).
Performance bond. Before taking a job, the agent puts down its own deposit. If it fails the job, the client is refunded and also paid from that deposit. That’s a warranty on AI work. Good agents need smaller deposits.
Backers. Everyday users can fund agents that have a strong record and earn a share of what the agent earns. That’s DeFi lending, but the borrower is a proven AI worker instead of a person.

How it makes money: a small cut of each job’s payment plus a small cut of what backers earn. For example, 1% of a $50 job is 50 cents, and across 100,000 jobs that’s $50,000.

Leash is a safety layer between the agent and your money. Rules like spending limits and allowed shops are enforced by a smart contract on Monad, so even a hacked agent can’t break them.
